My Cultural Food

Food and Flavour of My Culture

Traditional Pakistani Food

Biryani

Biryani is a popular Pakistani rice dish made with rice, meat and a variety of spices. It is mostly enjoyed at family gatherings and celebrations, however celebrations are incomplete without biryani.

Karahi

Chicken karahi is a traditional Pakistani dish made with chicken, tomatoes, spices and herbs. It is an important part of the culture when it comes to food.

Nihari

Nihari is a slow cooked meat dish with rich spices. It is often served with naan (bread) and is enjoyed as a traditional Pakistani meal.

Sweet and Special Occasions

Pakistani sweets are often enjoyed during celebrations such as Eid, weddings and family gatherings. They are commonly shared at every occasion.

Kheer

Kheer is a creamy rice pudding made with milk, rice and sugar. It is often served during celebrations and special family occasions.

Gulab Jamun

Gulab jamun is a sweet dessert made from soft dough balls soaked in sweet syrup. It is popular at celebrations and special occasions, such as Eid.

jalebi

Jalebi is a crispy, spiral shaped sweet soaked in sugar syrup. It is enjoyed as a dessert or treat during occasions.

Why Food Matters

Food is an important part of Pakistani culture because it brings families and communities together. Traditional recipes are passed down through generations and help keep cultural traditions alive. Sharing food is also a way of showing hospitality and creating memories with loved ones.
